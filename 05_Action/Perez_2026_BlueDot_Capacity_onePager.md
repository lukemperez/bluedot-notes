## Focus

I want to help state and local governments build the capacity to withstand a misaligned AI incident before one happens, not after. Little to no work has been done on security capacity below the national level, and when, not if, a misaligned agent causes real harm, our state and local governments will be in a reactive mode. My approach draws on war gaming and civic education: crisis simulations and tabletop exercises that move civic leaders and government officials past abstraction and towards concrete, practical thinking about the future, while bringing more people into the tent of AI safety.

- **Simulation design for AI risk.** I developed a plan for a crisis simulation that tests the assumptions state and local officials have about their capacity to mitigate harm from a misaligned AI incident. The design uses an identical scenario across teams with different levels of hypothetical capacity, mixes participants across party lines and civic roles (e.g., pairing a city council member with a local church leader), and follows the exercise with a reception and a second event to build lasting relationships among participants.
